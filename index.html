Our Story

A patisserie built around the pause between bites

Nuha opened as a small promise: pastry made properly, coffee pulled with attention, and a room worth lingering in. Warm marble underfoot, walnut overhead, and a kitchen visible enough to trust.

Every cake is finished the same day it's served. Every espresso is pulled to order. It's a small menu, kept that way on purpose.
